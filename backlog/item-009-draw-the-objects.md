# 9. Draw the objects as themselves

**Status:** blocked on [3](item-003-tgi-sections.md) · **Effort:** large

## What and why

An object draws as a coloured diamond with its type number in it. That is honest and readable, and it is as far as a marker can go: the map can say *there is a kind 0 / type 37 here* but not *there is a key here*.

Two things unblock it, in this order: [1](item-001-name-the-objects.md) gives the things names, and [3](item-003-tgi-sections.md) gives them geometry. With names but no geometry there is a good intermediate, a per-type glyph or silhouette drawn by hand, which is worth doing on its own and does not need the disc.
