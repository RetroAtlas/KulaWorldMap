# 21. One hash, written twice

## What and why

`seed` in [skins.js](../public/js/skins.js) and the opening of `phasesOf` in [motion.js](../public/js/motion.js) are the same hash of a cell and a face, written twice by two sessions a day apart from the same reading; they are meant to agree, since both stand for the dice the game throws per face, and nothing says so.

By [the folder's own rule](README.md) it would not earn a file on its own, since it is an hour's work; it is here so that whoever is next in those files does it rather than adding a third copy, and [item 19](item-019-the-wandering-balls-dice.md) and [item 20](item-020-split-the-renderer.md) both pass through it.

## Sketch

One hash, exported once, taken by both.
