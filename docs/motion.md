# How each thing moves

What the executable does to every object and every moving block each frame, read off the code of the NTSC-U release with `tools/mips.py`, and what each reading rests on. `python3 tools/kula_motion.py` prints every number below with the address it was read at and stops if an instruction is not the one it expects, and `--table` prints what the build ships under `motion` in `public/objects.json`: the rates by type and by kind of block in the game's units, the two cycles below as a frame program per phase, simulated from their routines and cut at one frame for all four phases so the game's spacing between them survives, and under `play` the level's own words, its clock, its endings, the words the end screen names them with, what an ending does and the fly-in's hold on the ball ([camera.md](camera.md)). The viewer draws everything that moves on the spot from that table, on a clock of sixty frames a second that starts at the level's first frame as the level opens, as play's does, and stands while the page is hidden, and what travels across blocks does so from the same table and the rules below, one game frame at a time; the Motion switch holds all of it at that first frame, except for a few things posed to show what they are. [level-format.md](level-format.md) is the same document for the level file and [ggi.md](ggi.md) for the meshes.

## The frame

**Everything below is per frame, and a frame is a sixtieth of a second.** The play loop at `0x80040210`-`0x800406c4` runs one level a pass (`0x80040690` calls `0x80040718`), and that routine loops a frame at a time from `0x80040aa0` to `0x80041c34`. Each pass waits once, at `0x80051d08`, for a flag that a callback installed through the system library before the loop sets (`0x8003fdb4`, installed at `0x80040a7c`), which every rate play has borne out puts at once a vertical blank; unless one of two words, `0x800a572c` and `0x800a592c`, is set, in which case the wait is `VSync(2)` and a counter at `0x8004188c` ticks by two instead of one, and nothing in the executable writes either. Play bears out that the level's clock ticks once a pass, 50 ticks to a second it shows: OBJ LEVEL's 99 seconds are then 4950 passes, and play counted 57 moves of a wandering ball in them, at 86 a move, where 99 seconds of sixtieths would have held 69. A cross-check comes from the level data: a moving platform's speed on the disc is 30 units a frame on all 37, and the loader at `0x80032a5c` scales it by 50/60 before use, which is what a game authored for a 50 Hz release does when it is built for 60. The rates that live in the code carry no such scaling, so on the PAL disc they may run at 50 a second or may have been retuned; this disc says nothing about that.

Angles are in 4096ths of a turn, the unit the sine table at `0x8005fb58` takes; distances are in units of which a block is 512; and one word, `0x800a33d0`, gates every update below with a test against zero. It selects a second view the shipped game never draws: it is a data word that loads as 0 and is never written, so the gates are always open.

## The things on a block

The routine at `0x80039200` walks every record of kinds 0 to 4, every slot of each, and every object whose `f4` is not zero, and both advances and draws it; the table at `0x80010290` sends each type to its own case. **An object's angles live in its own slot**: `f11`, `f12` and `f13`, which this document calls A, B and C, are set at load (`0x80035930`) to random values up to 4095 for every type the loader knows, so the phase of every pickup is random on every visit, and the map hashes it from the thing's cell so that it is the same on every visit here; the boost button alone starts at A = 16. Which angle turns which axis is named by the game as x, y and z, and its z is the axis [ggi.md](ggi.md) calls y, the normal of the face the thing stands on, since a coin's spin is its z. The bob some types have, and the catalogue star's circling, are an offset the routine adds to the object's position, and before it does it turns the offset by the object's own matrix (`0x80039cf0`), the one `0x80039ec0` builds from its face and facing, so a bob is along the face's normal on whatever face the thing stands, twenty units either way where a block is 512, and the circling is in the face's plane.

The variant a type draws is `f3` for every type, read by the draw dispatcher at `0x8002d878` as the model `type × 16 + row × 4 + f3` in the second table of the .GGI, the row a level of detail ([ggi.md](ggi.md)), which is why a coin's tier, a gem's cut and a teleporter's colour are all the same field, and why type 42 changes its shape by writing its own `f3`. Three more things happen in the draw and not in the walk: a teleporter, a switch or an exit whose `f4` is 1 turns a light on the faces round it over every 19 frames (`0x8002d424`, and "The devices' light" below); the exit's face shows the second quad of its model, the green cross, on every frame with no keys left (`0x8002c150`), the face being the last exit face the loader builds, which walks the lattice with `z` outermost and `x` fastest (`0x8002787c`, `0x800279fc`), as the exit the last key opens is the last the object loader meets, which walks the records in their order (`0x80035868`), so that where a level has more than one exit the two can be different exits; and a fruit's type is rewritten at load to the lowest of the five whose bit in the word at `0x800a5664` is clear (`0x80035664`), which reads as the first not yet collected and is the rule the map states as the level's number counted round the five. The loader also numbers each object it knows in the order it meets them and writes that into `f5` (`0x80035998`), the index of its runtime entry, so the number the disc holds there is not the one the game plays by.

The viewer steps a boost button's count as the game does, a frame at a time, and presses it with the ball at the level's start, measured from the point where the ball touches its face to the button's place on its own face. The game measures from the ball's position (`0x80039b74`), its centre, 100 off the face ("The ball's moves" below), to the middle of the button's block, the cell times 512 that the walk over the things writes before the button's case reads it (`0x80039294` to `0x800392a0`, `0x80039b6c`), and the two measures agree on every button on the disc but two: the six beside a start, on LEVEL 29, 30 (two), 42 and 91 and SIMON 8, sink flat as a level opens under either, as they do in play, and the two on SIMON 8 and LEVEL 119 that stand on a neighbour's far face are 724 units from the touch point, so the map leaves them up, and 624 from the centre to the middle of their block, so the game's measure sinks them ("Not settled" below). The block under the pointer and the selected block press a button too, each as the ball standing on that block's face the button stands on, since a block has no face of its own to give; so a button sinks as the pointer reaches its block or one beside it, springs back when the pointer leaves, and stays down while its block or a neighbour is selected, which on a touch screen is a tap.

## The ball

The ball at rest breathes. Its draw at `0x80031024` scales it by two matrices, one from a word at 700, which the level's load clears (`0x80035e34`) and a routine of the jump's alone writes (`0x80032558` to `0x8003259c`), not read here, and one for an idle swell that never stops (`0x800325c0`): an angle steps by 40 a frame plus a fortieth of what the level's time is short of 6000 ticks, the time being kept in fiftieths of a second (`0x80035f90`), and the ball is drawn 200·sin of it wider across the face, in 4096ths, and twice that shorter along the normal, a landing's squash riding on the same word ("The ball's roll" below); the first matrix is wider by an amount taken from its word and shorter by the same (`0x8003138c` to `0x8003139c`). Both scale the ball about its centre, and the draw then moves the centre along the normal by the ball's radius of 100 times what the two have taken off its height (`0x800314c4`), so its underside stays on the face and its top alone rises and falls. On a level with 99 seconds that is a breath every 62 frames, just over a second, quickening to one every 22 as the clock runs down; the squash is applied after the ball's roll and before its face's orientation, so it is always along the face the ball stands on.

## The ball's moves

**The ball keeps a frame of three unit vectors, a heading, a side and a normal, and a position, and reads the lattice about itself every frame.** Its record is at `0x800ba114` (`0x8003688c`): the heading at 12, the side at 20 and the normal at 28, each three halfwords of −1, 0 or 1, and the position at 60, in units of which a block is 512, lying in the cell whose 512 units hold it (`0x80037464` to `0x80037470`). The start lays the three through `0x80038178` from the face and the slot's `f2` ([camera.md](camera.md)), the normal being the face's outward normal (`0x80038260` to `0x800382b0`), and `0x800383ec` lays them as a matrix in 4096ths at 212, the side, the heading turned round and the normal its columns (`0x80038400` to `0x80038454`); the draw lays a second from the same three at 564, the side, the heading turned round and the normal turned round its columns (`0x800310dc` to `0x8003115c`), the frame the model is drawn in, which says nothing of the way the ball goes, and the body's own turn within that frame is a third matrix, at 596 ("The ball's roll" below). The position is the ball's centre: the start puts it on the face, at its cell's centre and 256 along the normal (`0x800361c8` to `0x8003621c`), and the state the ball rests in lays it 100 units off the face plane nearest it along the normal on every frame from the first (`0x8002f8e8`, `0x80037e8c` to `0x80037e98`, `0x80037ec4`), the cell boundary within 256 of the centre, so a centre as much as 356 below where it rests, 256 into its block, or short of 156 above it comes back to the same face, and the distance off the face is kept at 334 (`0x80037bc4`), the h the shadow fades by ("The shadows" below).

**Each frame the ball's routine runs the input routine, then the ball's state, reads the 27 cells about the ball, measures where it is within its cell, takes the move step and then runs the camera** (`0x80036540`, `0x80036838` to `0x800368dc`). The states dispatch by the state plus one through the table at `0x80010134` (`0x8002f8bc`, `0x8002f8d4`), −1 at rest, 2 rolling and 7 going round an edge, and `tools/kula_motion.py` holds the table to the three. Rolling, the state multiplies its speed by the heading and adds that to the position (`0x8002f9d8` to `0x8002fa10`), **so the heading points the way the ball travels**; the one place the move step subtracts the same product (`0x80030350`) is a frame's advance taken back, below. The 27 cells are read by `0x80037430` into words from 104 of the record (`0x8003762c`), a word a cell (`0x8003764c`), three along each axis of the frame (`0x80037798`), starting from the cell under the ball's own, one back and one over to the side (`0x800374a8` to `0x800374bc`): each word up adds the normal, each word on adds the heading and each word over takes the side off (`0x80037590` to `0x800375a8`), so the words step 4 bytes over, 12 on and 36 up, and the block under the ball's cell is the word at 120 (`0x800377a4`), the floor ahead of it at 132, the cell ahead at the ball's own level at 168, the two beside the block under at 116 and 124 and the two beside the block behind that at 104 and 112. A word holds −1, empty, where the lattice holds nothing or the cell lies at the lattice's edge or past it, at 0 or at 33 or beyond on any axis (`0x800375e0`, `0x800375fc`, `0x80037450`, `0x80037730`), and otherwise what the lattice holds, a style or a record, as it is (`0x80037688`, `0x80037694`), a moving platform's cell alone read otherwise (`0x800376c0`). So a block the ball can stand on or roll against is any cell the lattice fills, an invisible block, a crumbling block and the block a laser's end stands on among them, and a beam's cells, −1 or −2, read as empty.

**The ball's progress through its cell is measured from the edge behind it, so that 256 is the middle.** `0x80037a98` takes how far the centre is into its cell along each axis, its position plus 256 within 512 (`0x80037aa8`), and keeps the one along the heading at 336, taken from the cell's far side where the heading runs the other way (`0x80037b10`, `0x80037b2c`), with the last frame's at 344 (`0x80037af4`). The one along the normal is kept at 334 the same way, from the far side where the normal runs the other way (`0x80037bac` to `0x80037c5c`): how far along the normal the centre is into the cell it lies in, 100 at rest, starting over from nothing as the centre crosses into the cell above, with the frame's change along it at 326 (`0x80037d2c` to `0x80037d3c`). `0x80037d48` lays a progress by moving the centre along the heading from the edge behind it (`0x80037d6c`, `0x80037d74`). The ball has come to the middle of its cell where its progress is 256 or more and the last frame's was 256 or less (`0x80030e44`, `0x80030e4c`, `0x80030e58`, `0x80030e60`).

**The move step runs in the rolling state alone** (`0x80030264`), and first asks whether the ball has come to the middle of its cell (`0x8003026c`): there it stops, the centre laid at the middle, the speed nothing and the state at rest, where the forward request is gone or a turn is asked (`0x80030284` to `0x800302b0`), so the ball rests at the middles of cells and nowhere else. Else it asks whether the ball may roll on (`0x800302bc`, `0x80030dfc`): it may where the floor ahead is there, else where both cells beside the block under are empty, else only on ice, the word at 356 being 2 (`0x80030e04` to `0x80030e38`). Where it may not, a roll short of the middle goes on to it, a roll on ice would slide on past it (`0x800302dc`, `0x800302e8`), a branch never reached since the predicate refuses nothing on ice ("The blocks under the ball" below), and a roll already past it stops where it is, the frame's advance taken off the position again (`0x800302d4`, `0x800302f0`, `0x80030350` to `0x8003037c`). Where it may, two things can change the face the ball is on, and the roll on to the block ahead is what happens where neither does.

**A wall ahead takes the ball up it, with no arc.** Where the cell ahead at the ball's own level holds a block (`0x80030388`, `0x80030390`) and the progress has reached 413 (`0x800303a0`, `0x800303a4`), the move step lays the centre at 412 along the heading, 100 short of the wall and so touching it (`0x8003049c`, `0x800304b4`), and writes −1 into the word at 428 (`0x800304a0`, `0x800304a4`). The view routine `0x80034a50`, which the camera runs later in the same frame (`0x800368dc`, `0x80034380`), reads that word (`0x80034cdc`), turns the heading round into the normal and puts into the heading the normal it saved at 52 as the last ramp ended or as the start ran it once (`0x80034d00` to `0x80034d5c`, `0x80034de0`, `0x80034e14`, `0x80036260`). So the heading becomes the old normal, up the wall, the normal the old heading turned round, out of the wall, and the ball stands on the wall's face 100 off it, at a progress of 100 from the floor it left, and rolls on up to the middle of that face. The wall takes the ball whatever stands beside the block under; its one gate is that the roll was allowed at all, which asks that the floor ahead be there under the wall, or both cells beside the block under be empty, or the ball be on ice.

**An edge takes the ball round it, onto the next face of the block it was standing on.** Where the centre has passed the edge, the ball's cell having become the cell ahead, with no block under that cell (`0x80030500`, `0x80030508`), where the words at 104 and 112, which from there are the two cells beside the block the ball is leaving, are both empty (`0x80030510` to `0x80030528`), and where the last frame's progress plus the speed reaches 512, the cell's far edge, past which the progress itself has wrapped (`0x80030530` to `0x80030544`), the move step enters state 7 (`0x80030560`, `0x80030564`), lays the progress at nothing (`0x8003055c`, `0x8003058c`) and swaps the frame: it keeps the normal turned round at `0x800a4854` (`0x8003059c`, `0x800305a0`), puts the heading into the normal and that turned normal into the heading (`0x800305d8` to `0x80030600`). So the normal becomes the old heading, out of the block's far face, the heading the old normal turned round, down that face, and state 7 runs the centre round the edge and puts the ball back rolling (`0x8002fc44`), from where it rolls on to the middle of the new face. The two cells it asks after are the side rule's: going round an edge needs the floor ahead empty and both cells beside the block under empty, and an edge where a block stands beside the block under is refused twice over, by the predicate above and by the input routine.

**The input routine refuses the edge beside a neighbour before the roll starts.** It takes the pad into three requests, forward at 96, a turn at 92 and a jump at 100, and where forward is asked and no jump is, it drops the forward request where the floor ahead is empty and a block stands beside the block under on either side (`0x8003700c` to `0x8003705c`). At rest the forward request starts the roll where a block is under the ball and it may roll on (`0x8002f560`, `0x8002f698`, `0x8002f6a8`, `0x8002f6d0`), so a ball facing such an edge from rest does not move, and one rolling toward it stops at the middle of the last block, the request being dropped on every frame it stands there. A jump asked with it lets the request stand. On ice the predicate lets the ball roll on over such an edge, where nothing takes it round, into the cell ahead with no block under it.

## The ball's roll

**Each frame the ball's routine runs in one order: the level's clock, the pad, what moves and what can hurt, the ball's intents and state, the cells about it, its place in its cell, the move step, the landings, its contact with what it stands on, and the camera** (`0x800362f8`, `0x80036540`, `0x800366bc`, `0x800366f4`, `0x8003670c`, `0x8003679c`, `0x80036830` to `0x80036848`, `0x80036890` to `0x800368a0`, `0x800368cc`, `0x800368dc`, `0x800368e4`). The pad is not read while a countdown at 370 or 372 of the record runs, the first a device's, an ending's or the fly-in's wait ([camera.md](camera.md)), the second a run of frames the game rolls the ball for itself: before the pad, on every frame the count at 372 is not nothing, the routine takes one off it, asks the roll and drops the turn and the jump (`0x800364dc` to `0x80036504`), and the gate reads the count after, so the pad is read again on the frame it reaches nothing (`0x80036514`, `0x8003651c`, `0x80036524`); nor is the pad read while the ball stands on a boost button, type 10 in the word at 356 (`0x80036528`, `0x80036538`). The draw that follows (`0x80036958`) draws the ball and then the things on the blocks (`0x800369ac`, `0x800369b4`), and takes the cell of the point 256 along the normal from the ball's centre on each axis, and where that cell is 36 or more, or −2 or less, writes −3 into the level's outcome word, the ball lost (`0x800369bc` to `0x800369ec`, `0x80036a18`, `0x80036a5c`, `0x80036a64`). The view's target is the centre with a platform's travel added, written at 388 before the camera runs (`0x800368a0`, `0x8002f3d4`, `0x80034f38`, and [camera.md](camera.md)).

**The pad asks three things of the ball: the roll while Up is held, the jump while Cross is held, and a turn, left or right, as Left or Right is pressed** (`0x80036e20` to `0x80036e48`, `0x80036cac` to `0x80036cf4`), into the requests at 96, 100 and 92, a turn being 1 for left and −1 for right, all three of which it first puts at nothing (`0x80036b44`, `0x80036b98`, `0x80036b9c`), so a request made earlier in the frame stands only where the pad is not read. The pad is the console's digital word, Up 16, Right 32, Down 64, Left 128, Cross 16384, R2 512 and R1 2048, as held this frame, and a press is a bit held now and not on the last frame (`0x80036be4`, `0x80036bf0`, `0x80036cac`); a count at 364 runs while no turn does, and once it has passed 6 the last frame's pad is forgotten, so a held Left or Right presses again from the seventh frame after a turn ends (`0x80036c4c` to `0x80036c68`, `0x80036b10`), the 6 standing while the option at `0x800a33b4`, which the options menu toggles (`0x8004814c`, `0x80048154`), is set, and 0 otherwise, a held turn then repeating at once. A Left or Right pressed while a turn runs waits at 360 or 362 and is asked as the turn ends (`0x80036d18`, `0x80036d38`, `0x80036c84`). Nothing is read until the view routine has marked the ball live at 460, which the fly-in holds off for 69 frames (`0x80036c2c`, `0x80034a6c`, `0x80034348`). While the bouncy pill's word at `0x800ba33c` is not negative the jump is asked on every frame and Up and Cross go through a latch (`0x80036bbc`, `0x80036d48`), read below, and the Simon rooms' mode, 1 in the word at `0x800a33b0`, takes the pad as one move at a time against a stored sequence (`0x80036bf8`), not read here. The intents (`0x8002f3f4`) take the requests at rest, a jump asked with the roll as the forward jump and alone as the hop, else the roll, else a turn (`0x8002f5c0`, `0x8002f540`, `0x8002f560`, `0x8002f4e8`, `0x8002f500`); rolling, a jump asked is the forward jump at once, whatever the roll's request (`0x8002f488` to `0x8002f4a0`), and a turn may be asked during a hop (`0x8002f468`).

**From rest the ball reaches its top speed in six frames, and a block from rest takes 18.** The rolling state first moves the centre across toward the middle of its cell by 8 a frame where it is more than 8 off (`0x8002f934`, `0x80030e78`), then grows the speed at 312 by 6 a frame to 33 (`0x8002f9ac`, `0x8002f9bc`, `0x8002f9c4`) and moves the centre by it along the heading, so the speeds run 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 and then 33, 90 units in the first five frames and 33 a frame after, and the middle of the next cell, 512 on, is reached on the 18th frame, 0.3 s, where the move step lays the centre at the middle and drops the speed where the roll is no longer asked (`0x800302a8`); rolling on, with Up held through the middle, the ball covers a block in 512/33 frames, 15 or 16 by turns, 0.26 s. A roll over nothing, with no block under the ball's cell, between 101 and 427 of progress and not on a platform, drops the ball into a fall (`0x8002f944`, `0x8002f954`, `0x8002f958`, `0x8002f974`), as a ball at rest with the block under it gone is dropped (`0x8002f8f8`, `0x8002f910`), each by the word for the block under the ball's cell as the last frame's read left it (`0x8002f8f0`, `0x8002f93c`), the state running before the frame's read of the cells (`0x80036838`, `0x80036840`); a slide on ice is where a roll gets there.
