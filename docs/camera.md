# The camera

How the game frames a level: where its camera stands and looks while the ball rolls, how it flies in as a level opens, and the free camera the Japanese release gives the Select button, read off the code of the NTSC-U release with `tools/mips.py`. None of it comes from the level: the camera is a rule the executable applies to the ball's start and to the lattice. Angles are in 4096ths of a turn and distances in units of which a block is 512, as in [motion.md](motion.md). The ball's record is at `0x800ba114`, and a view is three rows, right, down and ahead, that the geometry coprocessor turns a point by before it adds the view's translation and divides by depth. [level-format.md](level-format.md) is the same document for the level file.

## The ball's frame

The start ([level-format.md](level-format.md)) calls `0x80038178` with the start slot's face and its `f2`, and it lays three unit vectors in the ball's record: up, the face's outward normal; ahead, the face's first tangent turned a quarter turn about the inward normal for each step of `f2` past 1, which is the rule an arrow's facing follows; and right. So the ball starts facing the way the map turns it, and the camera looks that way.

## The view in play

`0x80034a50` builds the view each frame from the ball's frame: its rows are right, the normal negated and ahead, pitched down by 250 (`0x80060f58` turns a view about its right axis), which is 22 degrees. Its translation puts the ball's centre, 100 off its face, with a platform's travel added (`0x8002f3c0`), 250 below the middle of the view and 800 ahead of the eye, so the eye stands 648 units behind that point and 531 above it, a block and a quarter back and a block up. The projection is the coprocessor's, with a screen distance of 160 (`0x800a33e4`, set through `0x800607a8` and written nowhere) on a screen 320 wide and 240 high with its middle at the centre (`0x8004ee90`): 90 degrees across and 74 from top to bottom. The ball's centre is 50 lines below the middle and a block is a third of the screen wide there. The rows the view is built from are the ball's side, normal and heading as the view last took them, with a yaw and a pitch of the record's own added, which a turn and a face change ramp from the old frame to the new over 19, 13 or 16 frames ([motion.md](motion.md)), and at rest both are zero. While the ball falls, or in a jump while its count at 404 is under 10, its last seven rows, over no block, a word at 504 of the record pitches the view further down: it grows each frame by a rate that rises by one a frame from 3, so by 4, 5 and on to 32, until the word passes 550, then by a rate that falls by one a frame to no less than 10 until the word reaches 650 (`0x80035050` to `0x80035128`), 57 degrees in all; once the ball is neither falling nor in those rows the rate is 3 again (`0x8003512c`), and on a frame the word has not grown, over a block or at rest, it comes back by 18 (`0x80035180`, `0x800351a8`). R1 held adds 21 a frame to the same word, to 650 (`0x80034f60`, `0x80034f90`), and R2 holds a word of its own that grows and comes back the same way and pitches the view up instead, bringing the eye in by half of it and the target up the screen by a third (`0x80034fd8`, `0x80035368` to `0x80035430`); the view is pitched down by 250 plus the first word (`0x800354cc`, `0x800354d4`). The same routine turns the ball's own frame, and not the view alone, as a wall ahead takes the ball up it and as a turn is asked ([motion.md](motion.md)).

## The fly-in

**As a level opens, and again on a restart, the start lays a cubic curve from 8 blocks behind the ball and 8 to its left to 100 units above the face it stands on, where its centre rests, and the view flies along it for 68 frames, 1.13 s, before the view in play takes over.** The level's start calls `0x80033e94` (`0x800362bc`), which takes the ball's heading, side and normal from its record at 12, 20 and 28, each a whole unit, and shifts them up by 12, to 4096, eight blocks (`0x80033ee4`, `0x80033f14`, `0x80033eb4`, `0x80033ebc`), and lays the curve's four points from the view's target at 388, which then holds the start's position on the face (`0x8002f36c` copies it from 60, laid at `0x800361c8` to `0x8003621c`) and which the first frame's rest state lifts 100 off the face, to the ball's centre with a platform's travel added ([motion.md](motion.md)): the last 100 along the normal from the target (`0x800341a4` to `0x800341bc`); the first 4096 against the heading and 4096 against the side from the last, 8 blocks behind and 8 to the left (`0x800341ec`, `0x800341f0`); the second a quarter of the way from the first to the last and a third of 4096 along the side, 3072 behind and 1707 to the left (`0x80034254`, `0x80034258`, `0x80034240`, `0x80034248`, `0x80034268`); and the third 2048 against the heading from the last, 4 blocks behind (`0x800342ac`, `0x800342b0`). The same routine puts the flight's parameter at nothing and its slowing step at 13 (`0x80033f34`, `0x80033f38`, `0x80033f2c`), two words of the globals, at `0x800a4a88` and `0x800a4a8c`, and not of the record.

**The parameter steps 16 a frame to 928 and once more past it, then 13, 12 and one less each frame, 5 at the end, 68 steps from 0 to 1025.** The view routine, `0x8003431c`, which the ball's routine calls last in its frame (`0x800368dc`), reads the parameter (`0x8003431c`) and takes the flight while it is 1024 or less (`0x80034340`, `0x80034344`) and the mode word at `0x800a33b0` is not 1 (`0x80034350`, `0x80034354`, `0x80034358`); it evaluates the curve at the parameter and then steps it, by 16 while it is 928 or less (`0x80034604`, `0x8003460c`, `0x8003463c`) and past that by the slowing step, which falls by one each frame to no less than 1 (`0x80034610` to `0x80034630`). So the view stands at the curve's start on the level's first frame, at 928 of its 1024 on the 59th and 944 on the 60th, then at 957, 969, 980, 990, 999, 1007, 1014 and 1020 on the 68th, and the parameter is 1025 on the 69th frame, on which the view in play runs instead; the step's floor of 1 is never reached. The curve is a cubic Bezier: `0x80033cbc` (`0x800343cc`) takes the four points, the parameter and a shift of 15 (`0x800343bc`), makes the unit 1 shifted up by two thirds of that, 1024 (`0x80033cc8`, `0x80033ce4`), takes the remainder, the unit less the parameter (`0x80033ce8`), and sums the points by the cubic's weights, the remainder's cube, three times the parameter by the remainder's square, three times the parameter's square by the remainder and the parameter's cube, each shifted down by 15 and the sum by 15 again (`0x80033d14` to `0x80033dc8`), so the point at the unit is the last point exactly.

**Over the flight the view turns from a quarter turn to the right to straight ahead, rolls from upside down to upright, and closes in from 14,000 units further out, so the level opens far off to the ball's left, upside down, and swings in behind it.** The flight's view is laid from the ball's frame and not from the rows the view in play holds: the heading, normalised (`0x80034428`), the side as the heading's cross product with the normal, normalised (`0x80034440`, `0x80034450`), and the normal turned round (`0x8003449c`), as the rows right, down and ahead (`0x80034470`, `0x800344a8`, `0x800344f0`); it is turned about its down axis by the parameter less 1024 (`0x800344f8`, `0x80034518`, `0x800610f8`), a quarter turn to the right at the start since a positive yaw turns a view left, pitched down by 250 as in play (`0x80034528`, `0x80034530`), and rolled about its line of sight by twice the remainder (`0x80034538`, `0x80034548`, `0x8003454c`, `0x80034554`, `0x80061298`), half a turn at the start. The curve's point, turned by that view (`0x80034570`), is put at the middle across (`0x8003458c`, `0x80034598`), 250 below the middle (`0x800345a8`, `0x800345b4`) and 800 ahead of the eye plus 14000 times the remainder over 1024 (`0x800345a0`, `0x800345b8` to `0x800345d4`, `0x800345e4`, `0x800345e8`, `0x800345f8`), 14,800 at the start, so it ends where the view in play puts the ball's centre, 250 below and 800 ahead, the curve's end and that centre one point, and the hand-over moves the target by nothing. The flight puts the free camera's two angles at 374 and 376 at nothing on each of its frames (`0x80034658`, `0x8003465c`, read at `0x80034690` and `0x800346b8`).

**Through the flight the ball's wait at 370 is held at 2000, and set to 1 as the parameter passes 1024, so the pad is dead for 69 frames and read from the 70th, and the platforms, the captivators and the hazards stand from the second frame to the 68th.** The flight writes 2000 into the wait on each of its frames (`0x800345fc`, `0x80034600`), over the 20 the start set, which the first frame's countdown had taken to 19 ([motion.md](motion.md)), and as the parameter passes 1024 writes 1 (`0x80034648`, `0x8003464c`, `0x80034650`, `0x80034654`), which the next frame's countdown takes to nothing; and it leaves the ball not live at 460 (`0x80034348`), which the view in play marks on its first frame (`0x80034a6c`) and the pad routine asks before it reads (`0x80036c2c`). So the pad routine is skipped on the flight's 68 frames and reads nothing on the 69th, and the keys work from the 70th, 1.17 s in, with the clock 70 ticks down. The ball's routine skips the platforms, the captivators' moves and the hazards while the wait is 1000 or more (`0x80036648`, `0x80036650`, `0x80036654`), so they stand from the second frame to the 68th, 67 frames, and run again from the 69th; on the first, under the start's 20, the platforms and the captivators' moves run, and the hazards too where the start's guard does not stand ([motion.md](motion.md)); meanwhile the clock ticks, first in each frame (`0x800362f8`), the captivators' reach is tried against where they stand (`0x8003679c`), the intents, the state and the landings run with nothing asked, and from the second frame the contact walk does not run, needing the ball live (`0x800368ac`, `0x800368b4`), which the start's own call of the view in play (`0x80036260`) left it on the first, where it runs unless the guard stands. The one mode whose word at `0x800a33b0` is 1, the Simon rooms', in which the level's clock does not run either (`0x8003b490`), skips the flight, so the view in play runs from the first frame and the start's 20 stands. The table ships the flight as `play`'s `flight`: the hold and the gate it holds at, the 1 it ends with, the parameter's end and steps and the frames flown, the yaw and the bank it undoes, the pitch, the depths and the curve's points as shifts along the ball's frame.

## The free camera

The routine that lays the curve, `0x80033e94`, measures the lattice too: the box round every cell it fills, the box's middle, and the distance from the middle to the furthest of them, plus 1200. While the ball's word at `0x800ba296` is set, which the Japanese release's Select button does and the cheat in [level-format.md](level-format.md) does on this one, `0x80034684` holds the eye that far from the box's middle and turns it with the pad. The level feeds this camera nothing either.

## The view at an ending

While the level's outcome word at `0x800a5e00` is set, by a death, the exit or the clock, or either of two more words the loop asks ([motion.md](motion.md) has the three), the loop runs `0x80036aa8` each frame instead of the ball's routine (`0x80040fcc` to `0x8004100c`), and the view it builds (`0x800347b8`) marks the ball not live, turns by three angles of its own that step −8, 3 and 10 a frame about the lattice's middle, the free camera's rotation and target (`0x800348c8`, `0x800348e4`, `0x8003490c`), the first a call to `0x80060a38` with the three, which builds its matrix as a turn about x after one about y after one about z (`0x80060b0c`, `0x80060b20`, `0x80060b34` and `0x80060b74`, the third column on either sign of the first angle), so the third angle turns the level about the lattice's upright and the first angle about the view's own across axis, and puts the eye the free camera's distance plus 1400 from it, 1000 further and nearer by turns as a fourth angle steps 12 a frame, a swing every 341 frames, 5.7 s (`0x80034920` to `0x800349c4`); on a level with no key the fourth steps 58 and the eye swings 3000 about 3400 further (`0x8003487c`, `0x80034a38`). The ball is not drawn while the outcome stands (`0x80036980`, `0x80036988`), and the level that follows, the same after a death, opens with the fly-in.
